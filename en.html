How I work: the “Recommend and Earn” case

First understand why customers drop off halfway — and only then build.

  1. Spotted the gap

    Many customers accepted the program terms, but fewer than half invited even one friend.

  2. Formed a hypothesis

    The barrier was typing a phone number by hand: not everyone remembers friends’ numbers or wants to enter them in the app.

  3. Tested it

    We built invitations into money transfers: when sending money to a friend, a customer invites them to the bank right away. Participants grew by 200% in a month — hypothesis confirmed.

  4. Shipped the solution

    Added personal promo codes to share in messengers and social media, plus individual promo codes for partner companies, so we and our partners bring each other customers.

After: with invites in transfers, almost 20% of visitors invite a friend.
